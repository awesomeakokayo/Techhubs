# TechSkillHub — Free & Open Source

TechSkillHub is a free, open-source technology learning platform.

## What is free

All learning tracks, roadmaps, guided learning, resources, quizzes, practice tasks, projects, AI learning paths, and course progress are available without paying for a course.

An account may still be required for authenticated features such as synchronized progress and certificates.

## Payments

The repository retains legacy payment and entitlement models for compatibility with historical data. New learners are not required to purchase access to learning content or guided paths.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome. Curriculum improvements should follow the TechSkillHub World-Class Curriculum Standard and should favor authoritative resources, practical learning, meaningful assessments, portfolio-oriented projects, and globally relevant skills.
