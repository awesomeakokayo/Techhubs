# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TechSkillHub is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source community.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Welcome learners and contributors at different experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or participants from project spaces when behavior violates this standard.

Serious or sensitive concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than discussed publicly.
