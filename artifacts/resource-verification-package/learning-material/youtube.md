## Stage 1 — Channel Strategy

**Objective:** Choose a viable channel niche and define a clear proposition.

**Success criteria:**
- Research demand and competition
- Define the audience
- Set up branding

### Instruction

Pick the right niche before creating anything. Evergreen topics with search demand and low competition are the target.

OBJECTIVE
Choose a viable channel niche and define a clear proposition.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Research demand and competition
• Define the audience
• Set up branding

WORKED EXAMPLE
Define your channel. Step 1: choose one niche and one specific viewer. Step 2: write the promise — what the viewer gets from subscribing. Step 3: choose three content pillars backed by search or audience evidence. Step 4: write the pitch a viewer would repeat to a friend in one sentence.

COMMON MISTAKES
• Choosing a niche with no evidence of demand
• Copying a competitor exactly

## Stage 2 — Content Production

**Objective:** Produce content efficiently while keeping quality and originality.

**Success criteria:**
- Write a script with a hook
- Source footage and voice responsibly
- Design a thumbnail

### Instruction

Build a repeatable production system. Automation channels live or die by consistency and quality.

OBJECTIVE
Produce content efficiently while keeping quality and originality.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Write a script with a hook
• Source footage and voice responsibly
• Design a thumbnail

WORKED EXAMPLE
Produce three videos. Step 1: write a hook for the first five seconds. Step 2: structure the body around one idea. Step 3: end with a call to action. Step 4: record click-through and retention for each and decide what to repeat.

COMMON MISTAKES

• Thumbnails that promise something the video does not deliver

## Stage 3 — Growth & SEO

**Objective:** Grow through packaging and analytics rather than hoping for luck.

**Success criteria:**
- Apply SEO basics
- Optimise titles and thumbnails
- Read analytics honestly

### Instruction

Publishing is only half the work. Learn how to get YouTube to recommend your videos.

OBJECTIVE
Grow through packaging and analytics rather than hoping for luck.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Apply SEO basics
• Optimise titles and thumbnails
• Read analytics honestly

WORKED EXAMPLE
Optimise one video for search and click. Step 1: put the search phrase in the title naturally. Step 2: write a description that adds information, not keywords. Step 3: add chapters. Step 4: design a thumbnail that is readable at 120px. Compare impressions and click-through before and after.

COMMON MISTAKES
• Chasing views over watch time
• Changing metadata without recording the result

### Worked example

Optimise one video for search and click. Step 1: put the search phrase in the title naturally. Step 2: write a description that adds information, not keywords. Step 3: add chapters. Step 4: design a thumbnail that is readable at 120px. Compare impressions and click-through before and after.

### Common mistakes

   - Number of uploads

## Stage 4 — Monetization

**Objective:** Understand realistic monetisation paths.

**Success criteria:**
- Explain the Partner Program
- Evaluate sponsorships and affiliates
- Assess digital products

### Instruction

Multiple income streams make a channel sustainable. YPP is the start, not the end.

OBJECTIVE
Understand realistic monetisation paths.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Explain the Partner Program
• Evaluate sponsorships and affiliates
• Assess digital products

WORKED EXAMPLE
Plan monetisation honestly. Step 1: list the revenue options that fit your niche. Step 2: check each one's eligibility requirements. Step 3: pick the one that best matches your audience and content. Step 4: state the realistic timeline and the metric you will watch, without over-promising.

COMMON MISTAKES
• Assuming monetisation starts immediately
• Accepting sponsors that conflict with audience trust

### Worked example

Plan monetisation honestly. Step 1: list the revenue options that fit your niche. Step 2: check each one's eligibility requirements. Step 3: pick the one that best matches your audience and content. Step 4: state the realistic timeline and the metric you will watch, without over-promising.

### Common mistakes
- Assuming monetisation starts immediately
- Accepting sponsors that conflict with audience trust
