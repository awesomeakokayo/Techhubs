## Stage 1 — Marketing Foundations

**Objective:** Frame marketing work around the customer journey and a target audience.

**Success criteria:**
- Map the customer journey
- Define a target audience
- Choose meaningful metrics

### Instruction

Understand the full picture before specializing. Great marketers understand business goals, not just tactics.

OBJECTIVE
Frame marketing work around the customer journey and a target audience.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Map the customer journey
• Define a target audience
• Choose meaningful metrics

WORKED EXAMPLE
Write a positioning statement. Step 1: complete the template — "For [audience] who [problem], [product] is a [category] that [benefit], unlike [alternative]." Step 2: add one proof point (a number, a testimonial). Step 3: name one differentiator you can defend. Step 4: read it aloud — if a rival could say the same sentence, it is not positioning.

COMMON MISTAKES
• Targeting "everyone"
• Measuring vanity metrics with no link to revenue

### Knowledge check (3 questions, server graded: yes)

1. What is conversion tracking?
   - Counting website visits
   - **✔ Recording when users complete a desired action such as a purchase or signup**
   - Measuring page load time
   - Counting social followers
   - _Answer:_ Recording when users complete a desired action such as a purchase or signup · _Why:_ Conversion tracking records completions of the actions that matter. · _Level:_ understand

2. Why should you not stop an A/B test the moment one variant leads?
   - It costs money
   - **✔ Early results can be noise; the result may not be statistically meaningful**
   - It is against the rules
   - It slows the site
   - _Answer:_ Early results can be noise; the result may not be statistically meaningful · _Why:_ Testing only until a favourable result appears produces false conclusions. · _Level:_ evaluate

3. Which is a well-formed A/B test hypothesis?
   - "The new design is better"
   - **✔ "Changing the button colour from grey to green will increase signups because contrast draws attention"**
   - "Let us try something"
   - "Users prefer blue"
   - _Answer:_ "Changing the button colour from grey to green will increase signups because contrast draws attention" · _Why:_ A good hypothesis states the change, the expected effect and the reason. · _Level:_ apply
