## 5. Knowledge checks and answer keys (internal)

### Stage 1 — Editing Fundamentals (3 questions)

1. What is a J-cut?
   - · A hard cut
   - **✔ Audio from the next clip starts before its picture**
   - · A cut with no transition
   - · A cut to black
   - _Why:_ In a J-cut the incoming audio leads the incoming video (the audio shape resembles a J). · _level: understand_
2. Why level audio before final export?
   - · It changes the video resolution
   - **✔ So dialogue is consistently audible and nothing clips or is buried**
   - · It reduces file size
   - · It adds captions
   - _Why:_ Consistent audio levels keep speech intelligible across the edit. · _level: apply_
3. A video looks soft after upload even though it looked sharp locally. What is the most likely cause?
   - · The camera lens
   - **✔ Export settings (resolution/bitrate) too low for the platform**
   - · Too much colour grading
   - · The music choice
   - _Why:_ Under-spec export settings, or platform re-encoding, can soften a video. · _level: analyze_

### Stage 2 — Storytelling & Pacing (3 questions)

1. What is the purpose of a strong opening in a short video?
   - · To list credits
   - **✔ To earn the viewer's attention in the first seconds**
   - · To show the title only
   - · To increase the file size
   - _Why:_ Retention depends on giving viewers a reason to keep watching immediately. · _level: apply_
2. Pacing for a 60-second short compared with a 15-minute video should generally be…
   - · Identical
   - **✔ Faster and denser, with quicker cuts**
   - · Much slower
   - · Unchanged by format
   - _Why:_ Short-form needs a higher information and cut rate to hold attention. · _level: understand_
3. When should music be adjusted?
   - · Never
   - **✔ At story beats so it supports rather than fights the narrative**
   - · Only at the end
   - · To hide bad audio
   - _Why:_ Aligning music to beats reinforces pacing and emotion. · _level: apply_

### Stage 3 — Visual Effects & Graphics (3 questions)

1. What is the difference between colour correction and colour grading?
   - · None
   - **✔ Correction fixes exposure and white balance; grading applies a creative look**
   - · Grading is only for photos
   - · Correction is only for audio
   - _Why:_ Correction makes footage technically neutral; grading adds a creative grade. · _level: understand_
2. What is a lower third?
   - · A crop of the bottom of the frame
   - **✔ A text graphic in the lower portion naming a speaker or place**
   - · A type of transition
   - · A music track
   - _Why:_ A lower third identifies a person or location in the lower part of the frame. · _level: recall_
3. Sound design in editing primarily…
   - · Replaces dialogue
   - **✔ Reinforces on-screen action and mood with effects and ambience**
   - · Increases resolution
   - · Removes the need for music
   - _Why:_ Sound design heightens impact and presence through effects and ambience. · _level: understand_

### Stage 4 — Platform-Specific Editing (3 questions)

1. Which aspect ratio is standard for mobile Shorts and Reels?
   - · 16:9
   - **✔ 9:16**
   - · 4:3
   - · 1:1
   - _Why:_ Vertical short-form uses 9:16. · _level: recall_
2. Why add captions to social video?
   - · To increase file size
   - **✔ Much of viewing happens with sound off, so captions preserve comprehension**
   - · They are required by law in all countries
   - · To replace the audio
   - _Why:_ Captions keep the message accessible when audio is muted. · _level: apply_
3. Longer videos often retain viewers better when they…
   - · Start slowly
   - **✔ Deliver on a clear promise with a coherent structure**
   - · Hide the topic
   - · Use no chapters
   - _Why:_ A clear promise and coherent structure sustain long-form retention. · _level: understand_
