## 5. Knowledge checks and answer keys (internal)

### Stage 1 — Marketing Foundations (3 questions)

1. What is the customer journey?
   - · The delivery route
   - **✔ The stages a customer passes through from awareness to purchase and beyond**
   - · The company org chart
   - · The product roadmap
   - _Why:_ The customer journey describes awareness, consideration, purchase and retention stages. · _level: understand_
2. Why is a specific target audience important?
   - · To make ads cheaper
   - **✔ Because a message that speaks to everyone persuades no one**
   - · It is required by law
   - · To shorten the sales cycle
   - _Why:_ Clear segmentation lets the message resonate with a defined group. · _level: evaluate_
3. Which is the most useful marketing metric?
   - · Number of likes
   - **✔ Cost per acquisition relative to customer value**
   - · Total page views
   - · Number of posts
   - _Why:_ Metrics tied to acquisition cost and customer value guide decisions. · _level: analyze_

### Stage 2 — Core Channels (3 questions)

1. On-page SEO primarily involves…
   - · Buying backlinks
   - **✔ Optimising content and structure for the topics users search**
   - · Changing your hosting
   - · Posting daily
   - _Why:_ On-page SEO aligns page content and structure with search intent. · _level: understand_
2. Why is an email list valuable?
   - · It is free to grow infinitely
   - **✔ You own the channel and can reach subscribers directly**
   - · It replaces all other channels
   - · It guarantees sales
   - _Why:_ An owned list lets you reach an interested audience directly. · _level: understand_
3. A headline job in copywriting is to…
   - · List every feature
   - **✔ Get the right reader to continue reading**
   - · Use as many keywords as possible
   - · Be as long as possible
   - _Why:_ Headlines earn attention so the body can persuade. · _level: apply_

### Stage 3 — Paid Advertising (3 questions)

1. Search ads are strongest when…
   - · The audience is unaware of the product
   - **✔ The user has clear intent and is actively searching**
   - · You have no budget
   - · You want brand awareness only
   - _Why:_ Search captures existing intent; it works well for high-intent queries. · _level: understand_
2. Which metric shows the cost to acquire one customer?
   - · CPM
   - **✔ CPA (cost per acquisition)**
   - · CTR
   - · Impressions
   - _Why:_ CPA measures spend per acquisition and connects ads to outcomes. · _level: recall_
3. A campaign spends its budget with no conversions. What should you check first?
   - · Increase budget
   - **✔ Whether the landing page and tracking are working**
   - · Change the logo
   - · Post more on social
   - _Why:_ Before scaling, verify the funnel converts and conversions are tracked. · _level: analyze_

### Stage 4 — Analytics & Optimization (3 questions)

1. What is conversion tracking?
   - · Counting website visits
   - **✔ Recording when users complete a desired action such as a purchase or signup**
   - · Measuring page load time
   - · Counting social followers
   - _Why:_ Conversion tracking records completions of the actions that matter. · _level: understand_
2. Why should you not stop an A/B test the moment one variant leads?
   - · It costs money
   - **✔ Early results can be noise; the result may not be statistically meaningful**
   - · It is against the rules
   - · It slows the site
   - _Why:_ Testing only until a favourable result appears produces false conclusions. · _level: evaluate_
3. Which is a well-formed A/B test hypothesis?
   - · "The new design is better"
   - **✔ "Changing the button colour from grey to green will increase signups because contrast draws attention"**
   - · "Let us try something"
   - · "Users prefer blue"
   - _Why:_ A good hypothesis states the change, the expected effect and the reason. · _level: apply_
