# TechSkillHub — Plans and access: help & FAQ

Status: **draft copy for the informational transition (Phase 2).** It makes no
price or launch-date claims and hides draft prices. It mirrors the FAQ shown on
`/pricing`. Nothing here authorises sending email or publishing externally.

---

## What is changing?

Most learning stays free. Over time, a small number of the most complete tracks
are planned to become premium. You will be able to buy a **Track Pass** for one
track, or **Pro** for every premium track. Selected tracks are announced in
advance, and nothing changes for you until that transition is enabled.

## What stays free?

The free catalogue stays free: the large majority of tracks, their lessons,
progress tracking and certificates — together with a monthly allowance of AI
project assessments. "Premium" only ever applies to the small set of tracks we
explicitly name.

## How long does a Track Pass last?

A track-specific number of **calendar months** of access to one eligible premium track. The current eligible terms are Frontend: 6 months, Backend: 8 months, and Python: 5 months, aligned with each track’s published maximum learning estimate. The term is calculated on our servers from the moment your verified payment completes — not from when you opened checkout — and is unaffected by your device clock or timezone.

## Does a Track Pass renew?

No. A Track Pass is a **one-time** payment. It does not renew and nothing is
charged when the selected track’s access term ends. To continue premium access to that track
afterwards, you choose a new purchase yourself. (Pro, by contrast, is an optional
recurring plan you can cancel at any time.)

## Is Pro a subscription?

Pro is an optional recurring plan. Access continues to the end of the paid period
after cancellation. It is never started without your explicit action.

## What happens to my progress and certificates when access ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your progress, completed stages, submissions, assessment
history, achievements and **earned certificates** are preserved after a Track
Pass or Pro period ends. A completed track stays completed. Only new premium
assessment activity needs an active plan.

## Do I keep the access I have today?

Yes — while the transition is being prepared, access is unchanged. Any future
transition for existing learners is designed to include advance notice and a
grace period, and to preserve access to work already in progress. If we cannot be
certain about a learner's historical access, we keep their access rather than
guessing.

## Can I be charged or subscribed without choosing it?

No. Nothing is charged unless you start a purchase yourself. A Track Pass is
one-time; Pro is a subscription you can cancel. We do not convert free learners
into paying subscribers.

## Where can I get help?

Use the contact form, or the help links in any lesson. We will explain what is
included, how long access lasts, and how to continue from where you left off.
