## 5. Knowledge checks and answer keys (internal)

### Stage 1 — AI for Communication (5 questions)

1. Your manager needs a concise update from messy notes. What should you give the AI?
   - · Only "summarize"
   - **✔ Notes plus audience, purpose, decisions/actions, and desired format**
   - · A demand to be perfect
   - · No context
   - _Why:_ Relevant context and output requirements improve usefulness. · _level: understand_
2. Which recurring task is the strongest AI workflow candidate?
   - · A one-off ambiguous decision
   - **✔ A structured weekly report with stable inputs and review criteria**
   - · A confidential task using an unapproved tool
   - · An irreversible emergency decision
   - _Why:_ Repeatable, structured, reviewable tasks are strong candidates. · _level: apply_
3. You are given a new professional work task related to this stage. Which approach best demonstrates professional use of the stage competency?
   - · Use the first AI output if it sounds confident
   - **✔ Define the task, success criteria, and constraints for the professional work problem before choosing how AI should help.**
   - · Add more adjectives to the prompt until the answer feels professional
   - · Let the AI decide the success criteria after producing the output
   - _Why:_ Professional AI use starts with the problem, constraints, and definition of success. The tool or prompt comes after the work is framed. · _level: apply_
4. Which evidence would give you the strongest confidence that someone actually understands this stage?
   - · Whether the output is polished enough to avoid review
   - · Whether the prompt was longer than the previous one
   - **✔ Look for evidence that the learner can produce a structured work output, and can match an output to its audience rather than merely repeat a definition.**
   - · Whether the model generated the answer quickly
   - _Why:_ Competence is shown through correct application, evidence, reasoning, and the ability to explain or improve the result—not by recognizing terminology. · _level: analyze_
5. A learner completes the stage task but cannot explain why the result is reliable. What is the most appropriate next move?
   - **✔ Ask the learner to identify the success criteria, inspect the relevant evidence or failure modes, and revise the work.**
   - · Accept it because the output looks good
   - · Give them the answer and mark the stage complete
   - · Move them to the next stage because AI is probabilistic anyway
   - _Why:_ A world-class pathway requires the learner to understand and evaluate the result, not merely produce an output. · _level: evaluate_
